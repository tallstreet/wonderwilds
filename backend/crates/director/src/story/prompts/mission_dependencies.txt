
Planning order is determined by dependencies, not by the order of entries in the conditions or capabilities arrays. Those arrays are vocabularies, not a suggested sequence.
Before producing the plan, check the capability that achieves each remaining condition. Every requirement must either already hold in the supplied state or be established by an earlier action. Follow prerequisites recursively: if achieving B requires A, and A is not already satisfied, put A before B. Keep useful prerequisite milestones even when they are not the final outcome. Do not place a prerequisite after a condition that depends on it.
Check the proposed sequence from the actual starting location, inventory and completedConditionIds. Account for travel, consumed resources and the effects of earlier actions; do not assume a future effect is already true. Preserve supported alternative orders only when there is no dependency between them. Finally verify that the ordered remaining milestones lead to the offered outcome. Return only the requested plan JSON, without analysis or extra fields.
